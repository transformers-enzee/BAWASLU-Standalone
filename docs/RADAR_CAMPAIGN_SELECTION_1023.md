# v102.3 selection of authentic Intelligence Inbox campaigns

Smart Radar now loads authorized campaign options through /api/source-lineage/inbox-campaigns and lets the officer choose a campaign within the page. The selected campaign drives the existing source-verified Inbox feed, summaries, topics and drilldowns. No manual campaign-ID URL editing or unauthenticated enumeration required. Historic pilot remains the explicit default until a user selects an Inbox campaign. No AI or crawl calls. This advances, but does not alone close, #13 integration acceptance.
