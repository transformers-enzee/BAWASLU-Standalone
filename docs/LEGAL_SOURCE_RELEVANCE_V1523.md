# BAWASLU v152.3 legal source alignment\n\nAdds a read-only, lexical legal-source alignment indicator to the existing answer-quality response without changing the four legacy readiness check categories. Only citations displayed in the answer to eligible regulation, SOP or guidance documents contribute. Contextual reports and uncited sources cannot hide mismatches. A missing topic term or unresolved structured election scope requires officer review. Matching terms only indicate lexical overlap, never legal truth, applicability, authority or currentness. Seven synthetic regression scenarios are enforced by the Docker staging build.\n
