# v152.5 Claim-local legal reference audit\n\nExplicit prescriptive legal statements require an eligible (REGULATION, SOP, GUIDANCE) citation within the same sentence, or an isolated next-line citation. Other sentences, contextual reports and rejected/unavailable citations never satisfy claim-local attribution. Officer steps, bilingual cautions and eight synthetic regression tests are included in the mandatory staging build. This remains an attribution heuristic and never asserts semantic entailment, legal applicability or case findings.\n
