# BAWASLU v153.0: Synthetic legal-answer signal benchmark

Offline benchmark of 36 developer-labeled synthetic legal-answer scenarios, including English and Indonesian examples, source eligibility, article attribution, missing citations, legal polarity, numeric/date claims, election scope, review state, source relevance, enforcement, and version metadata. Scoring is based on per-case explicitly expected and unexpected review signals. It does NOT measure model response quality or human-accepted correctness, nor does it substitute for actual official legal references. Admin-only GET /api/assistant-evaluation/legal-benchmark returns scores and bounded synthetic diagnostic results with private no-store headers; no real evidence is queried or sent. The admin-only AI Assistant QA panel runs it on explicit click and provides a local JSON export. Existing real-source sampler and independent human scoring workflows remain unchanged. No Supabase migration, no AI credits, no automatic review approval. Mandatory backend/frontend gates verify regression performance and access restrictions.
