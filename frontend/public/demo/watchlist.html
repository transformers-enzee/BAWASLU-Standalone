How is Watchlist different from Smart Radar?

Watchlist focuses on one selected candidate at a time and draws both own posts and mentions by others directly from Social Listening. Smart Radar shows the broader relevant intelligence picture from Intelligence Inbox, prioritizes findings, and manages official review decisions in Action Desk. Candidate tracking is not an investigation.
