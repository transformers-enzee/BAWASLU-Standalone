Modification for this application:
  Papua region province KODE_PROV and id attributes were normalized to
  distinct codes 91 (Papua), 92 (Papua Barat), 93 (Papua Selatan),
  94 (Papua Tengah), 95 (Papua Pegunungan), 96 (Papua Barat Daya).
  Original province geometries are not modified.

The visual choropleth counts only VERIFIED INCIDENT LOCATION mappings linked
to evidence, not inferred candidate constituencies, office addresses, or
the geographic location of the social media uploader.
